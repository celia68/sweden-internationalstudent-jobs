# Sweden Job Guide for International Students

A practical guide for international students looking for jobs in Sweden, written from my own experience as a student in Uppsala.

## Who is this for?

International students in Sweden who want to find part-time jobs, internships, or their first job after graduation.

## What's inside (planned)

- Where to look for jobs (Platsbanken, LinkedIn, etc.)
- How to write a Swedish-style CV and cover letter
- Part-time work rules and things to watch out for
- Lessons I learned along the way

## About me

I'm a Language Technology student at Uppsala University. This project is also my way of learning GitHub and building useful things in public.
