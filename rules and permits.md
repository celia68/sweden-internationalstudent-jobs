# Rules and Permits for Non-EU/EEA Students

*Last checked: 28 October 2026. This is a summary of official information, not legal advice. Rules changed significantly in 2026, so always confirm on the official pages linked below. EU/EEA citizens have different rules (right of residence), see [Migrationsverket](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/).*

## 1. Working while you study

New rules came into force on **11 June 2026** for first- and second-cycle (bachelor's and master's) students.

| Situation | What applies |
|---|---|
| Residence permit decision received on or after 11 June 2026 | Maximum **15 hours per week during semesters**. No limit in **June, July and August**. |
| Residence permit received **before** 11 June 2026 | The 15-hour cap does not apply for now. The new rules apply if you later apply to extend your permit. |
| Work in education, student representation, administration, a traineeship, research or artistic research | No limit, but the work must be at your own university or connected to your programme. |
| You have completed a programme of at least two semesters and hold the diploma | No limit while your permit lasts, as long as you don't apply for a new study permit at the same level. |

An application filed before 11 June but decided on or after that date is covered by the new rules.

Working more than the rules allow can lead to your permit being revoked or to problems when you extend it.

Source: [Migrationsverket: New rules for residence permits for studies in higher education](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/newsarchive/newrulesforresidencepermitsforstudiesinhighereducation.5.7e7ff7f619e5ace55a714.html)

## 2. Study progress and address

- To extend a study permit you must show acceptable progress. From 11 June 2026 the requirement for full-time studies is **37.5 credits in the first year** and **45 credits per year from the second year**.
- You must notify Migrationsverket of your address within 30 days, unless you are already registered in the population register.

Same source as above.

## 3. After graduation: permit to look for work (up to 1 year)

You can apply for a residence permit to look for work or explore starting a business.

**Main requirements**
- Your last permit was a residence permit for higher education in Sweden.
- You have completed the **entire** programme you were admitted to (at least two semesters) and passed **all** courses.
- You apply **before your current study permit expires**. If you haven't received all grades yet, apply anyway and add the certificate later.
- A valid passport, and you can support yourself for the whole permit period.
- Health insurance, if your total continuous time in Sweden is under one year.

**Key numbers (for applications in 2026)**
- Maintenance requirement: at least **SEK 10,656 per month**.
- Application fee: **SEK 1,500** for adults.
- Length: up to **one year** after bachelor's or master's studies.

**Documents:** passport copies, degree certificate or Ladok extract, bank statements, proof of health insurance where required. Documents must be in Swedish or English.

Source: [Migrationsverket: Look for work after completing your studies](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-extend/study/look-for-work-after-completing-your-studies-in-sweden.html)

## 4. Getting a work permit

From **1 June 2026** a new salary requirement applies to first-time work permits: the monthly salary must be at least **90% of the Swedish median salary**, and in line with collective agreements or common practice in the profession. It also applies to part-time work.

- Current figure (applications from 16 June 2026): **SEK 34,470 per month**.
- If you hold a residence permit as a student or researcher in Sweden when you apply for a work permit for the first time, you are exempt from the 90% rule for two years (or until the permit expires), but the salary must still reach **75% of the median (SEK 28,725)**.
- Migrationsverket can also reject an application because of problems with the employer.
- It is harder to switch from a student permit to another permit from within Sweden: as a general rule you must have completed a programme of at least two semesters.

The median is updated every June, so check the current numbers.

Sources: [Salary requirements for a work permit](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/word-explanations/salary-requirements-for-a-work-permit.html), [New rules for work permits from 1 June 2026](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/newsarchive/newrulesforworkpermitsfrom1june2026.5.4d7d222919d2a911cf74cf.html)

## 5. Personnummer and samordningsnummer

- Staying in Sweden **12 months or longer**: register with Skatteverket (Swedish Tax Agency) and get a **personnummer**.
- Staying **less than a year**: you can get a **samordningsnummer** (coordination number), also used for tax when you are paid.
- Start here: [Skatteverket: Moving to Sweden](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/folkbokforing/flyttatillsverige)
